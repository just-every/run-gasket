# Maintenance semantics

These rules define maintenance quantities, revision identities and calculations. They do not turn an estimate into a supplier receipt, a ledger posting or a physical-safety certification. Agent tools use company policy for their computed risk classes.

## ASSET-001

the site is the root; under it every register entry is a building, line, machine or component, and keeps its id and tag through every move. A parent ranks above its child in that order (a component may also hold components), is active, and is never the entry itself or anything below it; a stale read, a parent of the wrong level, a retired parent and a cycle are refused and write nothing, and an entry with active entries below it cannot be retired. Leads (and the owner) place an entry when they register it and move it later; moving an entry takes everything below it along, work, downtime, readings and history stay on the entries they were recorded on, and the move shows in the history of the entry and of both parents. An entry's roll-up covers it and everything below it: open work, ended downtime as the union of their outages so an outage they share counts once (the shared minutes are shown; entries down now are listed and their outage counts once it ends, as on each asset), and moved-in work cost (MIG-002) in parts, labor and other.

## ASSET-006

a lead or owner saves an asset's bill of materials as numbered revisions, never rewriting an earlier one (a stale revision number is refused): lines of parts with the quantity a repair needs and optional serial and model ranges the line fits, approved alternate parts per line each with its own optional serial and model ranges, and the tools the work needs, named. Serial ranges include both ends and compare in natural order (digit runs as numbers, letters ignoring case); model ranges use the same ordering, and both declared ranges must match. An asset with an unknown serial or model fits only an unrestricted range for that identity. A lead or owner records an optional model with the asset identity; corrections retain the previous model in identity history, and older clients that omit the model preserve it. The asset page and every work order on the asset show the current revision with each part's and alternate's available stock (PART-002), whether it fits the asset's serial and model and which lines are short, and earlier revisions stay listed. Reserving (PART-002) accepts an alternate the current revision lists for that line only when it fits the asset's serial and model, otherwise a site-wide approved substitute, and refuses with a visible reason a part or alternate outside either declared range or a substitute neither approves; that refusal is the warning. Existing reservations remain attributable commitments when identity or BOM applicability changes; every new reservation rechecks the current identity and BOM, with a revision fence in its atomic stock check. Historical BOM reads retain each revision’s original quantities, restrictions, author and time.

## PLAN-006

every member who can read a work order or an asset reads its comments, in the order they were posted; the owner, leads, maintainers and supervisors post them and an agent does not. A comment is text of up to 2,000 characters with up to four photos (PNG, JPEG, WebP or HEIC, up to 1.5 MB each), may be marked a shift handoff summary, and mentions active people chosen when it is posted; each mentioned person gets one in-app notice on the Notices page that links to the comment on its record. Only its author edits a comment and every version stays readable with who and when; no door deletes a comment, so approving, canceling or merging the record keeps its conversation, which can continue. A comment someone else wrote is unread for a person until they open the record's page or mark its thread read through the JSON door; an edit never makes a read comment unread, and a mention notice is unread until its comment is read. Every comment is an internal note.

## PM-008

the Automation page and JSON doors show the last scheduler trigger, the next UTC quarter-hour trigger, current calendar/meter due-event coverage and the latest executions. The owner pauses or resumes each current automatic job (PM generation, inspection routes, work chasing and document-expiry reminders) with a reason and numbered grant revision, pauses or enables an individual PM schedule with its revision, and runs catch-up under the revision they saw. Pausing retains due dates, issued work and source records; a last successful job more than 45 minutes ago is visibly missed even while paused. A read-only preview of a PM schedule, paused or enabled, uses the ordinary due evaluator but writes no work, notices, purchasing records, audit or execution rows. Each attempted job records its source (cron, reading or catch-up), grant revision, input facts, start, end and completed, paused or failed outcome; stale catch-up is a retained failure with no work. Automatic work audit entries and notices reference that job and its revision; PM work also keeps its schedule revision and due occurrence or reading, routes keep their stops, chasing its numbered rule, and document reminders their expiry facts. Automatic writes recheck the current unpaused grant in their transaction. The owner cannot use a job name to authorize reviews, spend or unrelated actions. The current notice jobs save in-app notices synchronously; their running and failed job counts are shown, while external email/push delivery is explicitly unavailable, never a zero provider backlog or an invented send.

PM attempts have a fifteen-minute execution lease, measured from actual execution time rather than a delayed scheduler event time. Health and execution records mark an expired running attempt as interrupted. A valid, unpaused PM execution first atomically reconciles expired attempts from their original committed work audit entries and links each to the recovery attempt. The originating scheduler run is retained as failed with its committed work count and the interruption error. Recovery retains the interrupted input and grant revision, records committed occurrences without replaying them, and evaluates due work under current inputs and authority. Every automatic mutation checks the attempt state and lease as well as the current grant; a superseded callback cannot write effects or overwrite its recovery record. Preview and stale-grant requests never claim recovery. This recovery covers primary-site PM issuance; it does not establish provider delivery, additional-site operation or comprehensive estate recovery.

## PM-009

leads publish numbered, immutable task mappings between higher and lower schedules, including schedules on linked assets, with a reason for the equivalence. Every lower task maps to a higher task of the same type, unit, limits and choices; the mapping pins both procedure revisions and cycles are refused. On the Programs page a lead previews and applies a specific pair of occurrences with the same due day. The higher work must be independently approved and closed; each mapped task needs a passing result or its required photo, never an exception or an unanswered, failed or conditional non-applicable task. The lower work must still be untouched and open. A missing mapped task leaves that occurrence and its due date unchanged. Applying the equivalent records both work versions, procedure revisions, mapping revision, review and original result/photo references, retains the lower work as cancelled with a visible satisfaction link (never as separately performed maintenance), releases its reservations and advances only its schedule by its ordinary cadence. A satisfied occurrence cannot be reopened or applied twice; later mapping or procedure revisions do not rewrite earlier satisfaction evidence.

## FIELD-005

evidence may be marked before or after the work and carry the time it was taken (site local time, never ahead of now) besides its upload time, and records the door it came through (`web` or `api`; offline capture is `sync` under FIELD-002). An annotation is a box, ellipse or arrow placed in percent of a photo's width and height (two decimals) with a note, drawn over the photo where its pixel size is known (PNG, JPEG); the original file and its SHA-256 never change. A technician signature is given by the assignee or a collaborator as themselves (the signed-in member); a customer signature is recorded on that person's session by the name the customer gives, with an optional drawn mark kept with its SHA-256, and is labelled as a name as given, not verified; a supervisor signature is given by a member with the review right who did no work on it, on submitted work, for the version they review. Every signature binds to the work order's version at signing and never changes the work; signing any other version is refused as stale. Any later change to the work makes technician and customer signatures stale, and submission waits until each such signer signs the current version; earlier signatures stay on record as superseded. A signature confirms the work record at that version and is labelled as not an equipment certification. FIELD-002 and FIELD-003 carry offline capture, offline signatures and upload provenance under these same rules before they ship.

## PART-002

a lead or owner approves one part as a substitute for another (that direction only) and can withdraw the approval, and keeps repair kits, each a named fixed set of parts and quantities (a different mix is a new kit). A planner (lead or owner) reserves to open work one part, or every line of a kit at once, each line filled by the part it names or by an approved substitute for it, never an unapproved one; a work order holds at most one open reservation per part, and a kit is reserved whole or not at all. A part's reserved stock is, over its open reservations, what each still needs (its quantity less what its work order has been issued of that part, net of returns, never below zero), and available is on hand less reserved; a reservation is refused unless every part it touches stays at or above zero available, checked in the same atomic write, so competing work cannot reserve the same last unit, and issuing to work never takes stock reserved for other work. A physical count can still leave a part short, shown as negative available. A reservation ends, kept with who ended it, when and why, when a planner releases it with a reason or when its work order is canceled, merged into another or approved and closed; reservations do not lapse by time. Reserving never moves stock: on hand changes only by receipt, issue, return and count. Gasket keeps one shelf location per part and one site today; PART-001 allocates reservations across bins and storerooms and ADMIN-002 across sites before they ship.

## PART-004

a lead or the owner plans a count of one shelf location's parts and/or chosen parts for one counter (an active owner, lead or maintainer); the count sheet never shows its counter the expected quantity while the count is open (the parts register still shows on-hand to its readers). Gasket reconciles rather than freezes: a count stands for the shelf at the moment it is submitted, each line keeps the on-hand and movement sequence then, and its variance is counted minus that expected quantity, posted onto whatever is on hand when it posts, so movements during and after the count are kept, never undone. A shortage needs the reason lost or damaged and an overage found; a matched line needs none. Each posted variance is valued by PART-007 at the stock's average when it posts. The owner sets the approval threshold in USD: variances valued at or above it ($0.00, the default, means every variance) wait for a lead or the owner who did not count them, and smaller ones post at submission as the counter's movement. Approval is refused as stale once another count of that part has posted after the line was counted, and refused when posting would leave on-hand below zero; a rejection needs a reason and posts nothing. Each posted adjustment is a count movement attributed to the person who posted it, carrying its reason, value and count number, and is in the audit (ADMIN-006) and the period reconciliation (PART-007). Until stores roles exist (ADMIN-003) leads and the owner act for stores staff and the stores supervisor. Direct physical counts (GAS-03) stay as published.

## PART-005

until PART-001 and ADMIN-002 add bins and sites, each part has one recorded stock location, and leads and the owner maintain immutable numbered policies for that location: minimum, maximum, reorder quantity, safety stock, active vendor, cost center, accountable active owner purchaser, calendar lead days and human-entered USD unit price, freight, sales/use tax and tax note. Available is on-hand less outstanding reservations; at or below minimum plus safety stock the quarter-hour worker drafts the larger of maximum minus available and reorder quantity, less outstanding linked deliveries, never below zero, with one open document per part. Read-only preview creates no purchases, notices, audit or execution rows. The draft pins its policy revision and creates an attributed in-app notice for the owner and leads; the notice is labelled stored in-app, with no external alert claimed. PM-005 inherits external email/push delivery before shipping it. Open documents show age as elapsed whole 24-hour days, exact remaining delivery and quarantine quantities, remaining stock shortage against maximum, and a due day equal to opening site-local day plus lead days; due on or before today prompts retained stores follow-up. Receipts and returns use BUY-004: quarantine is delivered but unavailable, and supplier returns reopen replacement shortage. Repeated workers neither duplicate the open document nor the same follow-up occasion. A buyer-edited PO revision is flagged for review rather than guessed to buy the same part. Automatic sending requires a separate immutable vendor/cost-center envelope proposed by the owner and explicitly approved by a different active owner or supervisor, never the policy author, envelope author, PO initiator or revision author. Its inclusive site-local period and per-order/period USD ceilings apply in addition to BUY-003 budgets; a claim consumes its amount even after uncertain or rejected submission, with no automatic retry or refund. The linked immutable PO vendor and cost center must match the current policy and envelope. Automatic sending also needs live BUY-003 threshold authority with no manual steps or emergency decision; otherwise the draft waits for ordinary independent purchasing decisions. Envelope ceilings and consumed USD amounts are visible only to the owner and supervisors with the review right, including in worker-history inputs; leads read vendor, cost center, dates and approval state and select an approved envelope by id without those amounts. Policy unit costs retain BUY-001/002 visibility. Revoking the envelope or ending its independent approver's authority stops new claims while retaining open orders. The owner pauses, previews, resumes and catches up under PM-008, and every automatic record keeps the worker job, grant revision and source facts; REPORT-009 defines the new measures and source doors.

## PART-007

Gasket's disclosed policy is moving-average cost, kept in whole US cents, and every stock movement keeps its own signed value beside its quantity, so stock value is the sum of movement values and no later event rewrites one. A receipt is valued at its unit price times its quantity, rounded half up to a cent, plus the freight and sales tax entered on that receipt, landed wholly on its part; a receipt without a unit price takes the part's current vendor price, and with neither it is unpriced. An issue takes the stock's average (stock value times quantity over on-hand, rounded half up; the last unit out takes exactly what is left). A return credits the work order's own issued value for that part in the same proportion, never today's average, so a work order's parts cost is what its issues were taken at less what its returns credited. A count variance is valued at the average, or with nothing on hand at the latest priced receipt's landed unit cost, else $0.00. Setting or changing a part's vendor price (leads and the owner) revalues no stock and no work. The period reconciliation (JSON and CSV) covers site-local dates from and to, both included: per part, opening (every movement before the start of the first day), received, issued, returned, count adjustment and closing quantity and value, the average unit cost and the number of unpriced movements, with a total row; it is downloaded by the owner and by the leads and supervisors who download asset history and the ledger handoff (ASSET-007). Work orders show each part's consumed cost and the asset history shows each issue's and return's value. Movements recorded before valuation existed and moved-in opening stock carry no value and are counted as unpriced, never as a cost.

## PART-009

the stores report (page, JSON and a source-movements CSV) is read by leads, supervisors and the owner over site-local dates from and to, both included (the last 90 days by default), and names its site. Stocked parts are those with stock on hand at some point in the period; a stockout is a movement in the period that leaves a stocked part with nothing on hand, each event naming that movement and its work order, and the stockout share is parts with a stockout over stocked parts; parts out of stock at the period's end are listed. Demand is issues less returns in the period, by quantity and the value PART-007 kept on them, per work order and per asset, with open reservations as committed demand. Slow stock is on hand at the period's end with no issue in the period; dead stock is on hand then with no issue in the 365 days ending then. Turns are issued less returned value over the average of opening and closing stock value, which are the PART-007 reconciliation's totals for the same period (two decimals, not annualized, none at a $0.00 average). Count accuracy is direct counts (GAS-03) and planned count lines (PART-004) in the period that found exactly the expected quantity, per part and in total. Opening stock moved in from MaintainX is listed apart from native receipts (part of balances, never of demand), every movement in the download is marked native or moved in, and moved-in work history costs stay in asset history, outside demand. Percentages have one decimal, rounded half up.

## BUY-001

until ADMIN-003 supplies the buyer role, the owner acts as buyer and manages suppliers, catalog entries and cost centers; the owner, leads and maintainers submit requisitions, and existing internal members read the purchasing queue and supplier records. A supplier keeps its named contact, email and phone, US ship-to street/city/state/ZIP, recorded sales-tax, buyer use-tax or exemption treatment (an exemption needs its basis), payment terms, lead time in whole calendar days and active status. Catalog SKUs name a part, service or external repair, its unit and an optional quoted USD price in whole cents; an unknown price stays unknown. Supplier edits use numbered revisions and keep attributed before/after facts in audit. A requisition names an active supplier, active cost center and active owner as accountable purchaser, has part/service/repair lines with positive quantities exact to three decimals, and may link work, an asset, or both (then the work must belong to that asset); work supplies its asset when no asset is given. It keeps the supplier facts it requested against and shows Pending approval: it neither authorizes spend, sends an order nor receives stock. Disabling a supplier refuses new requisitions while retaining earlier ones.

## BUY-002

until ADMIN-003 provides the buyer role, the owner acts as buyer and existing internal members read quotes, orders and original attachments. Buyers record immutable supplier quotations and select among compared quotes with a reason, then save an order with a sequential PO number; every edit creates a new numbered immutable revision and invalidates any earlier approval for the new revision. Quantities are exact to three decimal places, USD unit prices are whole cents, each extended line rounds half up to a cent, and vendor payable is the sum of extended lines, freight and recorded sales tax; buyer use tax is shown separately and included in the disclosed commitment, never added to vendor payable. Tax treatment and exemption basis are human-entered, never a tax determination. A supervisor or a different owner approves the exact revision, never its initiator or revision author. With no eligible independent approver the PO page states that it is blocked and directs the owner to People and access to invite a supervisor; it offers no self-approval exception. BUY-003 adds budgets, multi-step limits, delegation and emergency override to this independent revision approval before it ships, including a threshold policy that may permit small orders to skip manual approval. The buyer submits the approved immutable snapshot and its attachments to its recorded vendor email through installation mail; one claimed send is retained per revision, including after a crash or uncertain result, and no replay automatically sends it again. Provider acceptance is labelled mailbox delivery unconfirmed; local development suppression says no vendor email was sent, and refusal or uncertainty remains visible. Vendor acknowledgment is a separately buyer-recorded contact, reference and note, labelled not independently verified, never inferred from provider acceptance. Disabling the supplier refuses new drafts, approvals and sends while retaining old versions, approvals, submission outcomes and acknowledgments.

## BUY-003

until ADMIN-002 and ADMIN-003 add multiple sites and purchasing roles, the owner manages one site's policies and dated delegations, and active owners and supervisors supply independent decisions. A current immutable policy revision defines a USD budget for inclusive site-local dates, for the whole site or one cost center; both scopes apply together, and periods cannot overlap within one scope. The original authorization day books a commitment (threshold authorization books at send), never the promised delivery day. Required steps are the named active owner/supervisor at each threshold at or below the commitment, ordered by dollars, then site before cost center, then entered position; each earlier step must be approved first. A zero threshold asks for manual approval on every amount. Below every configured threshold, the policy supplies authority without manual approval, honestly labelled; with no policy, BUY-002's independent approval still applies. Delegation has inclusive dates, a reason and immutable versions: its delegate acts in place of its named principal, both must retain eligible roles and neither may be the initiator or current revision author. Each decision pins its policy step, delegation revision and exact PO revision, and policy or delegation changes are re-evaluated before later decisions and sending. Currency other than USD is refused without changing the order; line or vendor edits create a new revision needing authority again. Atomic budget checks count each approved current revision once and every sent historic revision until an independent reviewer explicitly confirms its release with a reason; an unsent superseded revision ceases to be an obligation. Neither competing completions nor send can exceed a ceiling without a separately confirmed independent emergency decision naming its authority, reason and projected budget impact. Releases and overrides remain in attributed reports and audit, and never assert receipt, supplier agreement, bill, posted actual cost or payment. Missing independent authority blocks with a page explaining how the owner adds a supervisor or changes its named policy/delegation. Existing internal members retain PO/history access with step names, status, delegates and outcomes; budget values and the override report require the owner or supervisor review right, and editing policies/delegations is owner-only. The versioned measure registry and source-linked report follow REPORT-009, including visible excluded/released obligations and unapproved drafts. ADMIN-002 adds site scoping, ADMIN-003 its buyer/approver grants, BUY-004 and BUY-005 actual receipts/bills and BUY-006 automatic-order limits before they ship; none changes this commitment into an invented actual.

## BUY-004

until ADMIN-003 supplies stores roles, leads and the owner receive and inspect; existing internal members read receipts and original evidence. Each delivery pins an approved immutable PO revision; a superseded revision may receive only after a retained send submission, and an unsent current revision must still have live authority. Part lines match an existing part in the same recorded unit; conversions are not inferred. A delivery may split a PO line across named bins, lots and individual serials; a serial identifies exactly one unit and is unique for that part. Delivered quantities are exact to three decimals and cannot exceed ordered quantity less prior deliveries plus supplier returns. Quarantine is delivered but excluded from usable stock and stock value until released after inspection. Attributed inspection events release held quarantine or return held quarantine or available usable goods to the supplier, with quantity and reason; returns reopen outstanding replacement quantities. Usable supplier returns remove current moving-average value as negative receipts, while quarantine returns remove only the held value; neither asserts a supplier credit, bill or payment. Each delivery keeps a supplier-scoped receipt identity: an identical replay returns the retained receipt, and a changed payload under that identity is refused. Original packing-slip bytes and SHA-256 remain downloadable behind sign-in. USD extended delivery lines round half up to a cent; freight and sales tax use largest-remainder allocation by delivered line value (ties by PO line position), then quantity splits within a line, and all allocated cents reconcile. A zero-value delivery cannot absorb nonzero charges. Quarantine takes its proportional landed value rounded half up, with the remainder retained on usable stock; releases exhaust the exact held remainder. The receiving page and JSON door show each revision's delivered, usable, quarantined, returned and outstanding quantities and retained backorder notes, with source-linked versioned definitions under REPORT-009. PART-001 carries bin-level stock, reservations and counts, PART-003 downstream controlled issues/expiry, and ADMIN-002 cross-site receiving scope before those capabilities ship.

## BUY-006

until ADMIN-003 adds purchasing and planner roles, the owner acts as buyer and existing internal members read follow-up. The Vendor follow-up page and its JSON door follow each order's latest revision with a retained submission (accepted, locally suppressed, unconfirmed or uncertain; rejected, rate-limited and failed sends never reached the vendor and are not followed) and show its promised delivery day, each delivery's site-local day and receipt identity, the latest buyer-recorded acknowledgment (not independently verified) or none, and per line the ordered, delivered (quarantine included) and outstanding quantity, which is ordered less delivered plus supplier returns (BUY-004), exact to three decimals. An order is partly delivered while some but not all of it is delivered, and late while anything is outstanding and the site-local today is after its promised day, by whole calendar days; a completed order shows how many days after its promised day its last delivery came. Reminders run only under an owner-approved immutable numbered rule, none by default, and only while something is outstanding and the supplier is active: one acknowledgment reminder when no acknowledgment is recorded once the site-local days since the submission reach the rule's acknowledgment days (0 to 30), and one late reminder once the delivery is the rule's late days (1 to 30) past its promised day. Each is one email to the order's submitted vendor address through installation mail, claimed before submission and never resent, labelled with its provider outcome: provider acceptance is not mailbox delivery or vendor agreement, local development says no vendor email was sent, and uncertainty or refusal stays visible. A later submitted revision is followed afresh with its own occasions. A delivery that is the rule's escalation days (1 to 60) late with outstanding part lines while its linked work is still open is a parts blocker: one in-app escalation notice, linked to that work and the PO and shown in the work's chase, goes to the planners, who are every active lead (the owners when there is none); no external alert is claimed. The auto-buy limits are PART-005's independently approved envelopes: each caps every automatic order and the envelope's inclusive site-local period in USD, in addition to BUY-003 budgets, and an automatic order over either ceiling is not sent. Follow-up runs as PM-008's Vendor delivery follow-up job every quarter hour; the owner pauses, resumes and catches it up under its revision, the page and JSON door show exactly what the next run would do without writing anything, and pausing it or the replenishment job keeps every open order, its outstanding quantities and its records. Each reminder and escalation keeps its job, grant revision and rule number, the job keeps its input facts, and its measures follow REPORT-009. ADMIN-003 adds the buyer and planner roles, ADMIN-002 site scope and PM-005/INT-004 external delivery of escalations before they ship.

## REPORT-002

the report covers a period of site-local days, both ends included (the last 30 days by default), for the site (every line and every entry with an outage) or one register entry and everything below it (ASSET-001), and is read by leads, supervisors and the owner. An outage is downtime recorded on a work order; whoever records it marks it a failure stop, a planned stop or degraded operation (the asset kept running at reduced output), with its reason and an optional failure mode, and its latest classification counts (REPORT-003). Outages are clipped to the period; one still open counts to now and its entry is listed as down now. Production time is the site's declared weekly production hours (windows per ISO weekday ending by midnight, which leads and the owner add and remove; with none declared, every hour of every day), as they stand now, inside the period; the source timeline shows each outage's recorded and clipped window, its work order and line, and its minutes inside and outside production time. An entry's planned time is the production time inside a planned stop of it or of anything below it, and is left out of the availability denominator (production time less planned time). Its down time is the denominator time inside a failure stop of it or of anything below it, overlapping outages counted once with the shared minutes shown, and availability is (denominator - down time) / denominator. Degraded operation is listed on its own, does not lower availability and is not a stop (REPORT-003). Unknown coverage is denominator time that no hour meter on the entry or below it was reporting (from its first to its last good reading in the period; a stale feed reading, ASSET-005, is not good), shown in hours and never estimated or counted as up or down. Minutes are whole; hours and percentages have one decimal, rounded half up.

## REPORT-003

the report covers a period of site-local days, both ends included (the last 30 days by default), for the site or one asset, and is read by leads, supervisors and the owner. A stop is downtime recorded on a work order (degraded operation, REPORT-002, is not a stop); whoever records it marks it a planned stop or a failure, with an optional failure mode code (ASSET-004), and a lead or owner corrects that classification by appending a new one with a reason, never rewriting the stop or an earlier classification; the latest classification counts and every one stays visible with who and when. A qualifying failure is a stop that started in the period and is not classified as planned; planned stops are listed and left out of every measure. Operating time is what the asset's hour meter (a counter whose unit is h, hr, hrs, hour or hours; the first by name when there are several) counted between its first and last good reading in the period; the rest of the period is unknown coverage, shown in hours and never estimated. MTBF is operating hours divided by the failures that started inside that covered time; failures outside it, or on an asset without an hour meter, are excluded and counted. MTTR is the mean minutes from a failure's stop to back in service over failures that have ended; failures still down are excluded and counted. MTTA is the mean minutes from a failure's stop to the first acknowledgment of its work order (PLAN-002) at or after it; failures not acknowledged since are excluded and counted. The Pareto ranks failures by failure mode, most first, with each mode's share and the cumulative share, unclassified failures as their own row; a repeat failure is two or more failures of one asset with the same failure mode in the period. Hours, minutes and percentages have one decimal, rounded half up.

## REPORT-004

the report covers a period of site-local days, both ends included (the last 30 days by default), for the site and optionally one team (a schedule may name a team and an estimate, which the work it issues carries). Backlog is open, in-progress and returned work now, by age since it was raised (0-7, 8-30, 31-90, over 90 days) with the leads' estimated hours, and work with no estimate is counted and named rather than guessed; overdue is due before today and at risk is due within the next 7 days and not started; work awaiting review is counted on its own. A PM occurrence is a schedule and its due date (when issued more than once, its latest work order counts) and is judged once its due date has passed or its work is done: on time when submitted on or before its due date, otherwise late, overdue (still open), canceled, skipped (an approved skip) or not generated (due with no work ever issued); all of these stay in the denominator, occurrences not yet due are left out and counted. Planned/reactive compares preventive and corrective work approved and closed in the period; schedule attainment is the share of work due in the period, judged the same way, done on or before its due date. Percentages have one decimal, rounded half up.

## REPORT-005

the report covers REPORT-002's period and scope (site-local days, both ends included, the last 30 days by default; the site or one register entry and everything below it) and is read by leads, supervisors and the owner. Ranked downtime is the production minutes (REPORT-002) of failure stops; planned stops and degraded operation are left out and counted. Where failures of an entry and of an entry above or below it overlap, the shared minutes count once, for the failure that started first (then the higher entry), and the other shows them as duplicate time; failures of unrelated entries are separate losses even at the same time. Leads rank by asset, by line (the entry's nearest line, or "No line"), by failure mode (the latest classification, or "Unclassified") and by reason (the recorded reason, ignoring case and outer spaces), most minutes first, each row with its share and the cumulative share of the ranked total, its repeat failures (as REPORT-003 defines them), its repair cost and its lost production, and a drill-down listing each contributing failure with its counted and duplicate minutes and its work order. Repair cost is the sum of the USD cost entries (parts, labor, contractor or other, each with a note) that leads and the owner record on the failures' work orders, each work order once per row; entries are never edited, a negative entry corrects earlier ones and a work order's total never goes below zero; a work order with no entry is unknown, never zero. Lost production is counted minutes times the documented USD rate per production hour of the failed entry or, when it has none, of its nearest entry above with one; leads and the owner document a rate with its basis, or document that it is unknown, and the latest one of an entry counts. With no rate, or a rate documented as unknown, the loss is unknown and never estimated. A CSV download carries every ranked failure's inputs (recorded and counted windows, production, counted and duplicate minutes, repair cost, the rate with its entry and basis, lost production) and writes "unknown" where nothing is documented. Money is USD in whole cents, rounded half up; percentages have one decimal.

## REPORT-009

the Measure definitions page and JSON door keep a numbered, immutable definition for every current KPI, with its formula, site-local or recorded event-time policy, exclusions, freshness and public source-list door.
